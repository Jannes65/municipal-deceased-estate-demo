Cost gate framework

Used to prevent the municipality from spending legal and administrative costs on uneconomic files.

Go / hold / no-go decision matrix

Illustrative rules that can be changed per municipality and legal advice.

Proceed

High recovery logic

  • Property still in deceased name
  • Balance material
  • Debt not prescribed or defensible
  • Estate file absent or executor active
  • Cost-to-recovery ratio acceptable
Hold

Information gap

  • Property ownership uncertain
  • Estate search inconclusive
  • Balance split incomplete
  • Current occupier unresolved
  • Need further data before spend
No-go

Do not spend

  • No property or asset found
  • Low balance
  • Prescription risk too high
  • Estate insolvent or uneconomic
  • Legal cost exceeds likely recovery
