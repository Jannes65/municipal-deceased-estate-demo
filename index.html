AI use cases embedded in the workflow

Visible automation opportunities that reduce manual investigation and case preparation.

1. AI deceased-match confidence

Flags false positives and low-confidence DHA/account matches before they enter the estate workflow.

2. AI cost gate

Stops Deeds, credit bureau or tracing costs when debt value and recovery indicators are too weak.

3. AI collection-likelihood score

Combines debt, property, contactability, estate status, occupancy and last payment into a reasoned score.

4. AI evidence pack builder

Generates the recovery, escalation or write-off pack with audit trail and supporting documents.

5. AI communication assistant

Drafts executor letters, family SMS messages, call scripts and follow-up notes for approval.

6. AI transfer watch

Flags matters where property sale, transfer or clearance activity could unlock recovery.

AI recommendations remain decision support only. Human approval is required for formal claims, settlement, legal escalation, write-off and sensitive communications.
